11. Document diversity
Avoid returning 8 near-identical chunks from the same paper unless genuinely necessary.
Add simple configurable diversity logic after reranking.
Suggested default:
max_chunks_per_document = 3

Apply this after reranking.
Do not force one chunk per document. Sometimes several passages from one strong paper are appropriate.
Report when candidates are dropped for diversity.
12. Near-duplicate chunk handling
Dense retrieval can return overlapping or highly similar neighbouring chunks.
Add lightweight near-duplicate suppression using deterministic signals such as:
same document_id
adjacent chunk_index
very high normalized text overlap
identical or near-identical text hash

Do not use an LLM for this.
Do not over-aggressively collapse distinct evidence.
Preserve the highest reranked candidate.
13. Score handling
Preserve separately:
dense_score
rerank_score

Do not pretend they are on the same scale.
Final ordering should use rerank score when reranking is enabled.
If reranking is disabled:
dense score determines rank

Do not invent a fake combined score unless mathematically justified and documented.
