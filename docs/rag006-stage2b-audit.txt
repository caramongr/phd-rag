RAG-006 Stage 2B - audit of every accepted STRONG title match
=================================================================

Source of the accepted identities: docs/rag006-stage2b.txt (component
'title_search', 76 accepted STRONG matches). For each accepted identity the
audit asked a second, independent question: does the document really denote
that record?

Method
------
1. Accepted DOI re-resolved at its registered agency (Crossref, then OpenAlex
   for DataCite-style DOIs) and the agency's title compared with the
   DOCUMENT'S OWN local title.
2. Accepted DOI that is an arXiv DOI (10.48550/arxiv.<id>): the DOI's arXiv id
   compared with the arXiv id observed in the document itself (extracted from
   the document's own text, or present in its filename).
3. Accepted record with no locally comparable identifier: the accepted title
   compared with the local title, and an independent title search re-run to
   confirm the record is reproducible.
4. Cross-checks reported per row, for every row: year (tolerance +/-1) and the
   first-author FAMILY name, i.e. exactly the secondary evidence the enricher
   itself uses. 'unknown' means the evidence does not exist locally (missing
   local year, or a local 'author' that is a single boilerplate token such as
   DRP / ssrn / ilkkaw, which carries no family name) - not that it disagreed.

No value in the corpus was changed by this audit.

   identity: deduplication is by SHA-256 of the file, not by DOI.

     markup ('<scp>AI</scp> Literacy: ...').

     appearing as three non-ASCII characters in the merged title).
   Both identities are correct; only the merged title TEXT is worse than the
   local candidate. Recommended follow-up (not done in this run): strip
   XML/JATS tags and repair double-encoded UTF-8 in external values before
   merging.

   documents had no OpenAlex fallback result. Crossref is always consulted
   first, so no identity depends on a throttled query; the STRONG count is a
