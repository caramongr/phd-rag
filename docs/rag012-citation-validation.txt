====================================================================================================================
RAG-012 claim validation
====================================================================================================================
METHOD
  input           : the RAG-011 GenerationResponse (answer, source_map,
                    context with the exact source text, generation audit).
                    Nothing is regenerated in validation: original_answer is
                    preserved verbatim and validated_answer is rebuilt from it
                    by exact substitution of the [Sx] markers.
  deterministic   : marker exists | cited chunk is evidence-eligible | source
                    text present | chunk is not a reference list | quotations are
                    verbatim in the cited source | every numeral is present in
                    the cited evidence or derivable | metadata checks.
  support bands   : SUPPORTED when the cited evidence contains >=60% of the
                    claim's content terms and no quantified-unit merging is
                    detected; PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED when only 35-60% is present or
                    when two separately-sourced findings are joined in one
                    sentence; UNCERTAIN below 35%; UNSUPPORTED when a
                    deterministic check fails or nothing overlaps.
  policy          : unsupported claims are withheld from validated_answer and
                    listed in the warnings; partial and uncertain claims keep
                    their citation plus a visible annotation.
  semantic layer  : optional (Part C), used only for claims the deterministic
                    checks cannot decide, and recorded per claim.
